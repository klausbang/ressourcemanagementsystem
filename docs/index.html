  4. EUT (Unit Under Test) as a first-class entity, lab/equipment mutual-exclusion constraints, a reusable standard activity template, a project/discipline dashboard, a staff/customer calendar, and a per-activity change history, restructured SRS from customer discovery, and a small-phase roadmap through Phase 14 (Phases 8-13 complete; see PROJECT_PLAN.md).
  5. Cloud deployment to Railway after local stabilization.
